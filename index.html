About me

I'm an engineer by training who ended up where business needs, people and data meet. My work has moved from technical sales through project delivery into sales operations, and the thread is the same: understand how work really happens, then fix what slows people down.

I care about changes that help the people doing the work and that I can also explain in business terms: time, cost, quality and control.

Outside work I study Danish and perform magic, which keeps me curious about how people notice, follow and remember things.

How I work

The tools change from project to project. The approach stays the same.

See the friction

Stay close to daily operations and find where people lose time or information.

Structure it

Agree the process, the owner and the data before building anything.

Build the solution

Use the simplest tool that works: Excel, Power Query, Power BI, Copilot or Fabric.

Drive adoption

Pilot with real users, collect feedback, write the guide and keep improving.

The value is more than time saved: more capacity for customers, better data quality, faster answers and clearer ownership.

Sales Support Agent

Situation: As the team and product range grew, it wasn't always clear who owned a request or where to find a product answer.

What I did: Built an agent trained on our responsibility matrix, the product catalog I created and common FAQs. The new starter onboarding I redesigned is part of the same knowledge base. I tested it with the team and improved it over several rounds.

Result: Less confusion about ownership and routing, and a first stop for everyday questions, including for new colleagues.

Tender screening with Copilot

Situation: Checking incoming tenders for relevance took hours of manual reading every day.

What I did: Built a Copilot workflow that screens tenders for relevance and prepares summaries.

Result: Initial screening takes 5–10 minutes, over 90% less than before.

Price list updates and a live price app

Situation: Each price update meant up to a week of manual comparison, checks and reformatting.

What I did: Automated the update with Excel macros and Power Query. Then I took it further with a dynamic, always up-to-date Fabric app that is simple to use and works on mobile.

Result: Updates take about 30 minutes instead of a week, and sales can find prices and product information on the go instead of searching a huge Excel file.
